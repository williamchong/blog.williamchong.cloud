# Games I wrote or contributed to

- [HideDotSeek](https://hidedotseek.com) - Made in [GGJHK 2015](https://v3.globalgamejam.org/2015/games/hide-dot%E2%80%A7-seek). Simplistic hide-and-seek multiplayer io online game. Avoid getting caught and find the safe zone as a black dot. Catch the black dots as a red dot!
- [Over-bingo](https://williamchong.github.io/over-bingo/) - Made in [Game Jam Band v2](https://itch.io/jam/game-jam-band2/rate/3758685). A chaotic fusion of bingo, math, and frantic multiplayer action.
- [VTuber-Mask](https://williamchong.github.io/vtuber-mask/) - Made in [GGJHK 2026](https://globalgamejam.org/games/2026/vtuber-mask-3). You're a VTuber who just went live. Dangerous content keeps appearing — in your chat, on the stream screen, and even on your VTuber self. The game explores the dual meaning of "mask": masking inappropriate content and the VTuber persona as a mask.
- [Candy Snake](https://williamchong.github.io/candy-snake/) - Made in [Game Jam Band 3](https://itch.io/jam/game-jam-band3/rate/4893500). You're an old-fashioned candy maker and your snake is the pulled sugar strand — grow it, knead in dye, then chop it into candies before the children queuing at your shop window lose patience.
- [Hong Kong TaxiQ](https://williamchong.itch.io/hong-kong-taxi-q) - An arcade taxi game set in Wan Chai and Causeway Bay, built in Godot from Hong Kong government open geodata — real road topology and building massing, reconstructed until a Hong Kong driver can navigate it from memory.
